Common questions

Straight answers for oversize and pilot car planning.

When do I need a pilot car?

It depends on the state and your load dimensions. HaulPath checks each state on your route and shows when a pilot car or escort is required for your width, height, length, and weight.

Does it work without cell service?

Yes. Regulations stay available offline after they are on your device, so you can look things up mid-haul in dead zones.

What is free vs Pro?

One state of your choice is free. Unlock all 48 continental states with a one-time $9.99 Pro upgrade—no monthly subscription. Upgrade inside the iOS or Android app.

Is this a substitute for official DOT guidance?

HaulPath is a planning aid. Always confirm permits and escorts with the issuing state agency before you roll. See our Legal & Privacy page for full terms.

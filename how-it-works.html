How HaulPath checks a haul

You enter the load, pick the route states, and read what each jurisdiction requires—permits, escorts, and travel hours included.

1

Enter the load and tap the map

Input width, height, length, and weight, then pinch-zoom and tap every state on the route. HaulPath checks those specs against each state’s legal thresholds.

2

See what each state requires

Analyze the route to get permit flags, pilot cars, and escorts listed per jurisdiction—not a single nationwide guess. One state is free. Pro covers the full lower 48.

3

Browse thresholds for any state

Open the States tab for legal limits, travel hours, and source details. Data stays on the device after download, so it works offline.

4

Save the haul and come back to it

Name the analysis and keep it in Routes with the dimensions and states you entered, ready for the next dispatch.

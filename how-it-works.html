How HaulPath checks a haul

You enter the load, pick the route states, and read what each jurisdiction requires—permits, escorts, and travel hours included.

1

Enter load dimensions

Input width, height, length, and weight. HaulPath uses those specs against legal thresholds across all 48 continental states.

2

Select your states

Choose each state you will travel through. The app applies that state’s rules instead of a single nationwide average. One state is free. Pro covers the full lower 48.

3

See what is required

View permit needs, pilot cars, high-pole cars, and police escorts for the dimensions you entered. Each selected state gets its own result.

4

Read hours and thresholds

Open a state for travel time, daylight rules, and the legal limits for width, height, and axle weights. Data stays available offline.

Run the check on your next load

Free for one state. Pro is $9.99 once for all 48 continental states.

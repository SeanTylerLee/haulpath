How HaulPath checks a haul

State-by-state compliance at a glance. You enter the load, pick the route states, and read what each jurisdiction requires—permits, escorts, and travel hours included.

1

Enter load dimensions

Input width, height, length, and weight. HaulPath uses those specs against legal thresholds across all 48 continental states.

2

Select your states

Choose each state you will travel through. The app applies that state’s rules instead of a single nationwide average.

3

See what is required

View permit needs, pilot cars, high-pole cars, police escorts, and travel-time rules. Data stays available offline.
